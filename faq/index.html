Frequently Asked

Questions, answered directly

Can't find it here? WhatsApp or call — it's Wayne who answers, not a script.

What areas does ZensCars cover?

Newquay and the surrounding area as standard, with regular runs to Truro, Padstow, Wadebridge, St Austell, Perranporth, Cornwall Airport Newquay and Royal Cornwall Hospital (Treliske). Longer journeys across Cornwall and beyond are available on request — just ask.

How do I book?

WhatsApp or call 07376 299060 with your pickup point, destination and time. For anything time-critical, like an early flight or a hospital appointment, book as far ahead as you can.

Do you offer fixed prices or a meter?

You get a fixed quote before you travel, agreed on WhatsApp or by phone — so there's no surprise when you arrive.

Is ZensCars available early morning or late at night?

Yes — flights, hospital appointments and nights out don't keep office hours, and Wayne works around that.

How many passengers and how much luggage can you take?

The ZensCars vehicle is a spacious people-carrier with room for passengers and luggage together — mention group size and bags when you book so the right car's arranged.

Can I book a regular or recurring journey, like a school run?

Yes — school contracts and other standing bookings are a regular part of the ZensCars schedule. Get in touch to set one up.

Still have a question?

Message it straight to Wayne on WhatsApp and get a real answer, not a bot.
